二、听下面三段对话，每段对话后有一个小题。每段对话读两遍。
听第 1 段对话，回答第 6 小题。
W: Excuse me, do you know when the library closes today?
M: At half past five. But the reading room stays open until nine.
6. When does the reading room close today?
听第 2 段对话，回答第 7 小题。
M: Hi, Lucy. Are you coming to the school concert on Friday?
W: I'd love to, but I have to finish my history project first.
7. What will Lucy do on Friday evening?
听第 3 段对话，回答第 8 小题。
W: The printer in the computer room is broken again.
M: I know. Mr. Li said a new one will arrive next Monday.
8. What are the speakers talking about?
